# FreshSpace Cleaning - Project Specification

## 1. Project Overview
A modern, mobile-friendly one-page website for FreshSpace Cleaning, a professional cleaning service for homes and small offices in Lagos, Nigeria.

## 2. Core Goal & Primary Call to Action (CTA)
- Primary Conversion Goal: Get users to request a quote via WhatsApp.
- Phone/WhatsApp Number: +2348000000000 (Placeholder to be formatted for direct WhatsApp chat).

## 3. Design Tokens & Styling Rules
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary (Fresh Teal/Cyan): `#0EA5E9`
  - Secondary (Deep Slate/Navy): `#0F172A`
  - Accent (Clean Green for WhatsApp): `#22C55E`
  - Background: `#F8FAFC`
  - Card Background: `#FFFFFF`
- **Typography & Layout:**
  - Font Family: Clean Sans-Serif (Google Font: Inter or Outfit)
  - Mobile-First, Responsive Design (CSS Flexbox/Grid)
  - Smooth Scrolling for section navigation

## 4. Required Page Sections
1. **Header / Navigation:** Logo ("FreshSpace Cleaning"), Links (Services, Pricing, FAQs, Contact), and a top WhatsApp button.
2. **Hero Section:**
   - Strong Headline (e.g., "Spotless Homes & Offices Across Lagos, Delivered Without the Stress").
   - Subheadline & Short Intro.
   - Primary CTA: "Get a Instant WhatsApp Quote" button.
3. **Services Section (3 Core Services):**
   - Residential Deep Cleaning
   - Small Office & Commercial Cleaning
   - Post-Construction / Move-In Cleaning
4. **Why Choose Us Section:**
   - Vetted & Background-Checked Cleaners
   - Eco-Friendly & Safe Cleaning Supplies
   - 100% Satisfaction Guarantee
5. **Simple Pricing Packages:**
   - Standard Home Clean (e.g., From ₦25,000)
   - Deep Cleaning Special (e.g., From ₦45,000)
   - Small Office Care (e.g., From ₦60,000/month)
6. **Testimonials Section:**
   - 2-3 realistic reviews from Lagos residents/businesses (e.g., Lekki, Ikeja).
7. **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ Accordion):**
   - 3-4 common questions (pricing, bringing supplies, booking process).
8. **Contact Details & Footer:**
   - Lagos coverage areas (Ikeja, Lekki, Victoria Island, Yaba, etc.), phone number, email.
9. **Floating WhatsApp Booking Button:**
   - Fixed bottom-right sticky button linking to WhatsApp.
